Known limits

Read these before you buy. They are the reasons a file might not fit your use.

  • region is the portal's own location label and mixes towns, areas and postcode districts (for example Ab10).
  • Removed from the download: agency_name, contact_phone. Agency and contact details are not part of any file we sell.
  • Not collected for this source, columns kept empty to preserve the schema: energy_certificate, floor.
  • Amenity flags carry a value only where the listing advertises the amenity (parking 5.1%, elevator 0.5%, terrace 7%, garden 5%, pool 0.3%). Empty means not advertised, not confirmed absent.
  • The portal does not publish a floor area for every listing. On those rows area_sqm is an estimate from the bedroom count and price_per_sqm is derived from that estimate: neither is a measurement.
  • price_original equals price on every row: this snapshot carries no price-change information.
  • Asking prices span £1 to £100,000,000; outliers are kept as published.
  • Rows were collected between 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-03; values can change within that window.
  • No personal data: no private names, phone numbers or personal email addresses.
