Known limits

Read these before you buy. They are the reasons a file might not fit your use.

  • One-off snapshot, not a live feed. Latest periods: sale 2026-Q1, rent 2024-S2, census 2021.
  • Sale prices are INE transaction medians, not listing (asking) prices. No property listings are included.
  • INE does not publish medians where there are too few transactions or contracts; those cells are blank with status NULL_OR_NOT_APPLICABLE, never zero.
  • Census counts reconcile exactly with INE national totals; the build is deterministic and checksummed.
  • Short-term rental registry data is not included (no stated licence; mainland only).
