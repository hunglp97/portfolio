Known limits

Read these before you buy. They are the reasons a file might not fit your use.

  • Six schema fields (bathrooms, energy_certificate, latitude, longitude, agency_name, contact_phone) are not collected for this source and arrive empty. They remain in the file to keep the 32-column REAL-ESTATE-BASIC contract intact. If you need geocoding or agency attribution, please discuss prior to licensing.
  • Amenity flags (has_parking, has_elevator, has_terrace, has_garden, has_pool) carry the value only where the portal advertises the amenity. An empty cell indicates “not advertised”, not “confirmed absent”. Do not interpret empty as an explicit negative.
  • Every record is a sale listing; there are no rentals in this dataset. Asking prices span EUR 15,000 to EUR 12,500,000, median EUR 572,500.
  • Zero personal data (no private phone numbers, names, or individual email addresses). 100% public property catalog intelligence.
  • Some district names in region and city carry a character-encoding error in this snapshot (for example Distrito de SantarÃ©m instead of Distrito de Santarém). The values map one-to-one to the correct names, but expect to clean them before joining.
