Known limits

Read these before you buy. They are the reasons a file might not fit your use.

  • Coverage is partial. idealista.pt showed 135,193 sale listings on 2026-09-22; this file holds 101,868 (75%). Lisboa, Porto and Faro were split into small areas, but 12 of the 113 crawl areas stopped at the portal's limit of 1,800 results, so most other districts hold about 1,800 listings each rather than all of them.
  • Not collected for this source, columns kept empty to preserve the 32-column contract: agency_name, contact_phone, energy_certificate, latitude, longitude. bathrooms is a placeholder (1 in every row) and neighborhood repeats city.
  • Amenity flags (has_parking, has_elevator, has_terrace, has_garden, has_pool) carry a value only where the listing advertises the amenity. Empty means not advertised, not confirmed absent.
  • Every record is a sale listing. Asking prices span €1,000 to €48,000,000; outliers are kept as published.
  • Rows were collected over four days (2026-09-22 to 2026-09-25); a listing withdrawn during that window can still appear.
  • No personal data: no private names, phone numbers or personal email addresses.
