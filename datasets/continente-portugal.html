Data notes & known caveats

  • Standard supermarket retail catalog feed formatted according to GROCERY-PLP schema contract (31 fields).
  • Six optional taxonomy levels (category6 through category10) and store_id/product_std_code arrive empty as they are not exposed by the portal interface.
  • Price per unit (PPU) is captured for 82.2% of SKUs where measurable units (weight, volume, doses) apply. Items sold strictly by individual count without weight information leave PPU blank.
  • Promotional mechanics represent active shelf tags at the moment of catalog collection.
  • 100% public retail catalog intelligence. Zero consumer or personal data.
