Known limits

Read these before you buy. They are the reasons a file might not fit your use.

  • Books (category1 Livros) make up 18,806 rows (35.1%). Filter on category1 if you only want grocery and household products.
  • This is the online catalogue, not a store price list: store_id is empty and prices are the web prices.
  • Not published by the source, columns kept empty: store_id, product_std_code, std_type, category5 to category10, promotion_end_date. category4 exists for 2.9% of products.
  • Unit price (ppu, unit_type) is shown for 39% of products; items sold by the piece without a weight leave it blank.
  • additional_content holds one value for every row. 78 products have category1 "undefined" as published.
  • Promotion tags are the shelf tags at the moment of collection.
  • No consumer or personal data.
