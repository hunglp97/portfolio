What every file comes with

You see the real columns and real rows before paying. Gaps in the data are named on the product page, not discovered after purchase.

A free sample
10 to 40 real rows cut from the file you buy, with the same columns and encoding.
Measured fill rates
For every column, the share of rows that carry a value, measured on the delivered file rather than estimated.
Known limits, written down
Fields a source does not publish are listed as not collected. Empty never silently means zero.
A collection date
Each file is a dated snapshot. If you need it refreshed on a schedule, that is a custom feed.
Plain formats
CSV in UTF-8 with a semicolon separator; the Portugal data pack also ships Parquet.
No personal data
No private names, phone numbers or personal email addresses. Business listings only.

How buying works

No account and no subscription. You pay once and download the file.

  1. Check the sample

    Download the free CSV and read the schema. Fill rates are measured on the same file you buy.

  2. Pay on Payhip

    Card or PayPal through Payhip's checkout. Prices are in US dollars.

  3. Download right away

    The download link arrives as soon as payment clears. Invoices on request; a defective file is replaced or refunded within 14 days.
