What every file comes with

You see the real columns and real rows before paying. Gaps in the data are named on the product page, not discovered after purchase.

A free sample
10 to 40 real rows cut from the file you buy, with the same columns and encoding.
Measured fill rates
For every column, the share of rows that carry a value, measured on the delivered file rather than estimated.
Known limits, written down
Fields a source does not publish are listed as not collected. Empty never silently means zero.
A collection date
Each file is a dated snapshot. If you need it refreshed on a schedule, that is a custom feed.
Plain formats
CSV in UTF-8 with a comma separator; the Portugal data pack also ships Parquet.
No personal data
No private names, phone numbers or personal email addresses. Business listings only.

Before you buy

Short answers to what buyers ask first. The full conditions are in the terms.

Can I check the data before paying?

Yes. Every dataset has a free sample: real rows cut from the file you buy, with the same columns and encoding. The product page also lists every column with its measured fill rate and the known limits of the file.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No. Datasets need no account and no subscription: you pay once and download the file. Only the monthly report is a subscription, billed by PayPal, and you can cancel it at any time.

How do I pay, and when do I get the file?

Datasets go through Payhip's checkout, by card or PayPal, in US dollars. The download link arrives as soon as payment clears. Invoices are available on request.

What format are the files in?

CSV in UTF-8 with a comma separator. The Portugal data pack also ships Parquet.

How recent is the data, and can it be refreshed?

Each file is a one-off snapshot, and its collection date is shown on the card and on the product page. If you need the same source refreshed on a schedule, ask for a custom feed.

What if the file is not what the page described?

Write to sales@hlpdata.com within 14 days of purchase with your order details. A defective file, or one materially different from its description, is replaced or refunded.

Do the files contain personal data?

No private names, phone numbers or personal email addresses. Business listings only.

What am I allowed to do with the data?

Each product page has a licence and delivery section that says what the file may be used for. The full conditions are in the terms.
