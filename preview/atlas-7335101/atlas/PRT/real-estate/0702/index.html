What this snapshot measures

Asking prices for apartments and houses, as observed on 2026-10-03. This is a snapshot of the listed sources, not a census of the entire Portuguese market.

Listings are not deduplicated across sources. Counts refer to advertisements, not unique properties. A municipality with no observed listings is not evidence of an empty local market.

Price medians use homes with an available price; price per m² uses usable area, or gross area if usable area is absent. Samples below 30 observations are hidden. Grey areas have insufficient data.

Gross yield is a ratio of rental and sale medians from different listings, before expenses. Listing age and price reductions cover all property types and only sources exposing those fields. They do not measure completed sales or liquidity.

INE reference period: 2026Q1. Asking and transaction samples differ; their gap is not a valuation discount. RNAL registrations do not measure active accommodation.
