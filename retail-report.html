What each edition measures

Three measures for every category, on the shop's full online catalogue.

  • Own-brand share. The share of products in each category that are the retailer's own brand. Latest edition: 22.2% overall, 58.2% in Casa e Jardim.
  • Discount labels, two kinds kept apart. The PVPR label is a discount against the manufacturer's recommended retail price; a direct promotion is a cut on the shop's own price. Latest edition: 29.8% of manufacturer-brand products carry a PVPR label (68.0% in Limpeza); direct promotions are on 5.0% of manufacturer-brand and 14.5% of own-brand products.
  • Unit-price gap. Own brand's median unit price as a share of manufacturer brands', compared within the same third-level category and the same unit. Latest edition: 56.0% across 209 comparable groups.

What you receive each month

Sent by email on the day the edition is published.

  • The report page with the three measures for every category, in Portuguese (the language of the shop's categories).
  • An Excel table at three category levels, with the number of products behind every figure.
  • A product-level CSV: brand, category, list price, selling price and discount label for every product.
  • From your second edition on, the change against the previous month.

Coverage and limits

Read these before you subscribe. They are the reasons the report might not fit your use.

  • Retailers in the report today: Continente online. Another retailer joins an edition only after its full catalogue has been collected and checked; no dates are promised.
  • A snapshot of the website on one date each month: web prices, not store prices, and not sales.
  • Own brand means brands that a public source identifies as the retailer's. Look-alike brands without a source are listed in the report and not counted.
  • Collected from public pages, from the shop's own sitemap and within robots.txt. HLP Data is independent of every retailer in the report.
  • Table and file column names are in English; category names stay as the shop writes them.
