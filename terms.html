1. Operational Scope & Service Model

HLP Data ("we", "us", or "our") operates as an independent commercial web data provider offering structured alternative data feeds, standardized catalog datasets, and recurring delivery pipelines for commercial analytics, hedge funds, and enterprise business intelligence.

2. 100% Public Data Sourcing Standard

HLP Data exclusively extracts and compiles facts, figures, and listings that are freely and publicly available on the open web. We do NOT:

  • Circumvent paywalls, subscription access gates, or private user credentials.
  • Access non-public backends, unauthorized internal APIs, or confidential databases.
  • Collect data behind private user authentication.

3. Strict Zero-PII Policy (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

We strictly adhere to global privacy regulations including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Zero Personally Identifiable Information Guarantee:

Our extraction schemas (e.g. REAL-ESTATE-BASIC, GROCERY-PLP, FASHION-PDP) contain exclusively commercial and aggregate data: product titles, stock codes, pricing, specs, geocodes, and dimensions. We do not extract, store, or redistribute personal emails, telephone numbers, private identity records, or individual consumer profiles.

4. Ethical Crawling & Server Respect

Our automated ingestion engines are engineered for zero-impact crawling:

  • Artificial Jitter & Delay: Requests maintain pacing intervals between 1.0s and 3.0s to avoid placing resource strain on origin servers.
  • Zero DDoS Footprint: Strict concurrency caps prevent traffic spikes or performance degradation on target websites.
  • Clean Architecture: We do not inject persistent trackers, invasive scripts, or malicious payloads into target domains.

5. Licences by Product

The licence depends on the type of product you buy. The product page states which type applies.

  • Scraped snapshot datasets (data collected from public websites, e.g. Rightmove UK, Idealista Portugal, Continente, Farfetch): delivered for the purchaser’s internal market intelligence, research, benchmarking and analytical use. Redistribution, resale or re-licensing of the data requires a separate written agreement with us.
  • Official-statistics products (e.g. Portugal Area Intelligence): derived from Statistics Portugal (INE) data published under CC BY 4.0 and Direção-Geral do Território (DGT) CAOP data published under CC BY. You may use the data internally and you may redistribute work derived from it, including commercially, provided you keep the source credits listed in the ATTRIBUTION.txt file included in the download (also shown under “Sources & licences” on the product page) and indicate that the information was modified.
  • Custom feeds: scope, delivery, refresh frequency and usage rights are set in the written quote or agreement for that feed.

6. Refunds

Datasets sold on this site are digital downloads. If a file is defective or materially different from its description on the product page, contact sales@hlpdata.com within 14 days of purchase with your order details, and we will provide a replacement file or a refund.

7. Subscriptions

The Portugal Retail Brand Report is sold as a monthly subscription billed through PayPal. The price, the currency and any free first period are those shown on the report’s page at the time you subscribe.

  • Billing: PayPal charges the plan price every month until the subscription is cancelled. We never receive your card details.
  • Free first month: where the plan has one, the first charge is made at the start of the second month. If you cancel before then, you are not charged.
  • Delivery: within one business day of subscribing we email the current edition to the address on your PayPal account, and each new edition on the day it is published. There is at least one edition in every calendar month; if a month you paid for has none, that month’s payment is refunded.
  • Cancellation: you can cancel at any time in your PayPal account (under automatic payments) or by writing to sales@hlpdata.com. Cancelling stops all future charges; the editions already delivered remain licensed to you.
  • Refunds: if the report is materially different from its description, write to us within 14 days of your first payment and that payment is refunded. Payments for months in which an edition was delivered are otherwise not refunded.
  • Licence: each edition is for the internal use of the subscribing organisation. A trade association may pass the report and its table to its member companies. The product-level data file may not be resold or published. Figures may be quoted with the credit “Source: HLP Data (hlpdata.com)”.
  • Price changes: if the price of a plan changes, subscribers are told by email at least 30 days before the new price applies and can cancel first.
