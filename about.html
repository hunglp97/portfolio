What We Sell

  • Dated dataset snapshots: structured files compiled from public web pages, each tied to the date it was collected. See the dataset catalog.
  • Official-statistics data packs: ready-to-use files built from open official statistics, shipped with the source credits their licences require.
  • Custom data feeds on request: a scope, format and refresh schedule agreed in writing for your use case. See Custom Data.

Each product page states what the file contains, its date and which licence applies. The licence types are explained in our Terms.

How Buying Works

  • Datasets: purchases go through Payhip’s secure checkout (card or PayPal), and the download is available instantly after payment.
  • Monthly report: the Portugal Retail Brand Report is a subscription billed by PayPal. You can cancel at any time.
  • Custom work: paid against a PayPal invoice after you accept the written quote.
  • Invoices: available on request; email sales@hlpdata.com with your order details and billing information.
  • Refunds: handled as described in the Terms.

How we handle personal data is described in the Privacy Policy.
