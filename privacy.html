Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

1. Who We Are

This policy explains what personal data HLP Data ("we", "us") handles when you visit hlpdata.com, buy a dataset, or write to us. For any privacy question or request, email privacy@hlpdata.com.

3. Website Analytics (Umami, no cookies)

We use Umami Cloud, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to see how the site is used. Umami does not set cookies.

  • What is collected: aggregate page views (which page, the referring site, browser, device type and country) and anonymous events: clicks on buy buttons, sample file downloads and email links. No form content or email address is sent.
  • What is not done: no personal profiles, no tracking across other websites, and no advertising or ad targeting.
  • IP addresses: your IP address is not stored by us. Umami’s own handling is described in the Umami privacy policy.
  • How to opt out: use a content blocker that blocks cloud.umami.is. The site works the same without analytics.

4. Preferences Stored in Your Browser

Your theme (light/dark) and language choice are saved in your browser’s local storage (hlp_theme, hlp_lang). They stay on your device and are not sent to us. If you have not picked a language, the site uses your browser’s language setting; it does not look up your IP address or location to choose one.

5. Purchases (Payhip, PayPal, Stripe)

Dataset purchases are processed by Payhip, which takes card and PayPal payments through PayPal or Stripe. On some pages the Payhip checkout opens on top of our page using Payhip’s script; Payhip’s own privacy policy applies to the checkout. We never see your full card details. Payhip shares with us the order details needed to deliver and support your purchase, such as your name, email address, the product bought and the amount paid.

6. Emails and Custom Data Requests

When you email sales@hlpdata.com or privacy@hlpdata.com, we use your name, email address and the content of your message to answer your request, prepare quotes, deliver data and issue invoices. The custom data form on this site does not send anything to a server: it prepares an email in your own email app, and nothing is sent until you send that email.

We do not sell, rent or trade your contact details.

7. Hosting and Third-Party Resources

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Pages also load the Umami analytics script from cloud.umami.is, fonts from Google Fonts, icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and, on the home page, a chart library from jsDelivr; these providers receive your IP address when your browser fetches those files.

8. Your Rights and Contact

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you (for example, your emails and order records), subject to records we must keep for accounting purposes. Analytics data is aggregate and anonymous, so it cannot be linked back to you. Send requests to:

Email: privacy@hlpdata.com
