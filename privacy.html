Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Overview & Data Controller

HLP Data ("we", "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information received via our public website (hlpdata.com) and client communications.

2. Information We Collect

  • Direct Inquiries: When you email us directly at hung@hlpdata.com, we collect your name, business email address, and any project requirements you share to respond to your inquiry and deliver proposals.
  • Zero Tracking Cookies: We do not run third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or invasive analytics pixels on hlpdata.com. Standard web server access logs (IP address, user-agent, timestamp) may be logged ephemerally for security and DDoS defense.

3. How We Use Information

Any information provided to us is used exclusively to:

  • Communicate regarding data extraction contracts and pipeline deliverables.
  • Provision secure S3 storage bucket credentials for dataset delivery.
  • Generate commercial invoices and verify PayPal business billing.

We never sell, rent, or trade your contact details to third parties.

4. Data Security & Storage

Commercial datasets and client communication credentials are encrypted in transit via TLS/HTTPS and encrypted at rest within enterprise AWS S3 infrastructure with IAM role-based least-privilege access policies.
